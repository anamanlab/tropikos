> Companion to `LAUNCH_PLAN.md` (site go-live). This document is the **marketing growth plan**: how to be found, cited, and convert — in traditional search, AI search, and local search. Built from the `seo-audit`, `ai-seo`, `schema`, `analytics`, `attribution`, `conversion-tracking`, `site-architecture`, `content-strategy`, `cro`, and `local-seo` skills.

**Site:** https://tropikos.com.br/ (also workers.dev alias)
**Business:** Architecture + interiors studio, Recife/PE, est. 2021, serves Nordeste
**Primary conversion:** WhatsApp inquiry (G-7KB6VK4D5Y)
**Audience:** Homeowners/developers in Recife/PE seeking residential architecture, interiors, renovations, mixed-use

## 0. North Star & Measurement

### North-star metric
Qualified WhatsApp inquiries per month from organic + AI search (target: ≥1/week within 60 days of launch).

### Leading indicators
- Organic sessions (GA4)
- `whatsapp_click` / `phone_click` / `email_click` events (GA4)
- Search Console: impressions, clicks, CTR, average position for priority queries
- AI citation rate: manual monthly check of top 20 queries across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews
- Google Business Profile: views, calls, direction requests, reviews

### Attribution stance
- **Source of truth for conversions:** GA4 events + manual WhatsApp inquiry log (no CRM yet). When volume grows, add a simple spreadsheet log of inquiry → project won.
- **Model:** last-touch (defensible for short local cycles) reported alongside first-touch once enough data. Don't over-engineer attribution before there's volume.
- **Blind spots to name:** direct traffic (junk drawer), branded search (someone else created the demand), AI assistants (influence shows up as direct/branded). Use a post-inquiry "como você nos encontrou?" WhatsApp question to capture self-reported attribution.

## 6. Content Strategy

### Pillars (3)
1. **Projetos / Portfolio** — case studies with location, typology, scope, narrative. (Already the core.)
2. **Processo / Como trabalhamos** — how a project unfolds, from first study to keys. (servicos page has process; expand into standalone posts.)
3. **Lugar / Recife & Nordeste** — climate, materials, culture as design drivers. (Thought leadership, shareable.)

### Content types
- **Searchable:** use-case pages ("projeto de casa em Boa Viagem", "reforma de apartamento no Recife"), how-to ("como escolher um arquiteto"), location pages (if justified).
- **Shareable:** project stories, behind-the-scenes, material research, "making of" posts, process transparency.
- **Link-earning:** a "estatísticas de arquitetura residencial no Recife" roundup (curated stats) — high link potential per Foundation Inc. data.

### Calendar: 60/30/10
- 60% searchable (project pages, service pages, how-to)
- 30% shareable (stories, behind-the-scenes, opinions)
- 10% experimental (new formats, video, partnerships)

### First content pieces (post-launch)
1. "Como funciona um projeto arquitetônico do início ao fim" (serviços expansion) — searchable + AI-extractable.
2. "O que considerar antes de reformar um apartamento no Recife" — searchable.
3. Case study post per featured project (long-form, with photos, decisions, materials) — shareable + link-earning.
4. "Arquitetura no Recife: clima, sombra e ventilação" — thought leadership, shareable.
5. FAQ page: "Perguntas frequentes" consolidating common client questions — AI-extractable.
