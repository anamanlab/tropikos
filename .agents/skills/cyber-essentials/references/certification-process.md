# Certification Process, Pricing, Insurance, Procurement (as of September 2026)

## Basic Cyber Essentials (verified self-assessment)
1. Purchase via IASME or one of 400+ licensed Certification Bodies; assessment account on the IASME portal (accounts created from April 27, 2026 use the Danzell question set; earlier accounts finish under Willow within their 6-month window, closing ~late October 2026).
2. Complete the question set for the defined scope; a board member (or equivalent) signs the declaration — under Danzell this includes maintaining compliance through the certification period.
3. Assessor marks the submission; two automatic-fail conditions (cloud MFA missing where available; A6.4/A6.5 14-day update questions). Fail → free resubmission window of 2 working days; 6 months to complete after purchase.
4. Pricing (IASME set, + VAT): micro (0–9 staff) £320; small (10–49) £440; medium (50–249) £500; large (250+) £600. No 2026 price change announced.
5. Certificate valid 12 months; renew annually.

## Cyber Essentials Plus
- Independent technical audit, completed **within 3 months of the basic certification** (the verified self-assessment need not be repeated if CE-certified <3 months prior). Priced by quote (size/complexity).
- Audit content: authenticated internal vulnerability scans of a random device sample (typically ~10% of in-scope devices, covering each build type), external vulnerability scans of internet-facing services, all internet gateways, all servers reachable by unauthenticated internet users, plus malware protection tests and email/browser download defence tests. Remote or on-site.
- Danzell changes: a failed device sample triggers a retest with a NEW additional random sample; a second failure revokes the underlying verified self-assessment certificate; self-assessment answers cannot be adjusted once CE+ testing begins.

## Included cyber liability insurance (basic CE, opt-in)
Eligibility: UK/Crown Dependencies domicile, turnover under £20m, WHOLE organisation certified. Cover: £25,000 total indemnity — incident response helpline, liability, event management, extortion, regulatory defence costs/insurable fines, business interruption; £1,000 excess (£5,000 US/Canada); electronic theft/cyber fraud excluded. Underwriter: AIG UK; administrator: Sutcliffe & Co. Condition: keep automatically provided vendor updates enabled for critical business software. Paid uplifts to £100k/£250k available. No extra cost; opting out changes nothing else.

## Procurement mandates
- **Central government — PPN 014** (in force for procurements from February 24, 2025; replaces PPN 09/23; aligned to the Procurement Act 2023): departments, executive agencies, NDPBs and NHS bodies require CE or CE+ where contracts involve handling certain government information or ICT service delivery; equivalent controls must be accepted (s.56).
- **MoD**: Defence Cyber Certification (DCC, IASME-delivered, launched 2025) supersedes the DCPP Cyber Security Model; MoD has asked all defence industry partners to achieve DCC Level 0 by December 31, 2026. Cyber Essentials remains the baseline expectation in defence supply chains handling MOD identifiable information.
- **NHS**: suppliers accessing patient data complete the Data Security and Protection Toolkit (DSPT) annually; treat CE+ and DSPT as separate evidence lines and verify current NHS England requirements per contract.
- Large-enterprise flow-down: CE/CE+ increasingly contractual in private supply chains (IASME cites an 80% incident reduction across one insurer's 2,800-supplier mandate).

## Timeline planning (typical)
Gap assessment 1–2 weeks → remediation 2–8 weeks (MFA rollout and unsupported-software removal are the long poles) → basic CE submission + marking ~1 week → CE+ audit scheduled inside the 3-month window. Bidders should back-plan ~3 months from tender deadlines requiring CE+.
